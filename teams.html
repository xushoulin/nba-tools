About this data

Team cap hits and player salaries are real 2026-27 figures sourced from HoopsHype (2026-11-19). Only the largest contracts on each roster are listed, so the sum of listed salaries runs slightly below the official team cap hit shown on each card — the gap is made up of minimum-salary players, incomplete roster charges and dead money. All figures can be edited inside the salary & trade simulator.
