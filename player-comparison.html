Frequently asked questions

Where do I find the stats?+

Any box score source works — Basketball-Reference, ESPN, or NBA.com. Enter per-game averages for a fair comparison.

How is the overall edge calculated?+

Each category is normalized and averaged into a single score so you can see which player has the stronger all-around line.

Does it account for minutes?+

You can compare raw per-game numbers or enter per-36 figures yourself for a minutes-adjusted comparison.
