Frequently asked questions

What is PER?+

Player Efficiency Rating is a per-minute measure of a player's statistical production, normalized so the league average is 15. It rewards efficient scoring, rebounding, playmaking, and defense.

What is True Shooting percentage?+

TS% measures shooting efficiency accounting for three-pointers and free throws. It is calculated as points divided by twice the true shooting attempts.

How accurate is this calculator?+

It uses the standard published formulas. Results match Basketball-Reference style metrics closely for single-game or season totals.
