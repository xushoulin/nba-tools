Frequently asked questions

What is the NBA salary cap?+

The salary cap is the limit on how much a team can spend on player salaries in a season. For 2026–27 the cap is $164.961 million, the luxury tax line is $200.428 million, and the minimum team salary is $148.465 million.

Can a team go over the cap?+

Yes. Teams can exceed the cap using exceptions such as the Bird exception, mid-level exception, and by re-signing their own players. Going over the first apron ($209.015M) and second apron ($221.686M) triggers increasingly strict roster restrictions.

Is this calculator official?+

No. NBA Tools is independent and not affiliated with the NBA. Figures are for estimation and entertainment purposes.

Does my data leave my browser?+

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser. Nothing is stored or transmitted.
