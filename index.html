Frequently asked questions

Is NBA Tools free to use?
Yes. Every calculator and simulator on this site is free, with no paywall, trial or credit card. Everything runs in your browser, so there is no account to create.
Do I need to sign up or share any personal data?
No. NBA Tools does not require an account and does not collect your inputs on the server. Calculations happen locally in your browser, and only anonymous aggregate traffic statistics are used to keep the site running.
Where does the salary and cap data come from?
Contract figures are point-in-time 2026-27 snapshots from HoopsHype, combined with the official NBA cap, tax and apron thresholds. They were retrieved in November 2026 and change constantly, so treat them as estimates rather than official transaction figures.
Where do player profiles and award records come from?
Player biographies, jersey numbers, honours and season-by-season statistics come from ESPN public athlete endpoints. The honours comparison groups 31 recorded award names into 11 categories and shows counts plus seasons.
Can I use these numbers for official NBA transactions?
No. This is an independent, fan-run project. It is not affiliated with the NBA, its teams or ESPN, and the figures are not an official source for contracts, cap space or statistics.

Data sources: player bios, honours and statistics from ESPN; salary snapshots from HoopsHype; cap thresholds from the NBA's published 2026-27 figures. Last retrieved November 2026.
