Frequently asked questions

How is trade value determined?+

You assign each player a 0–10 value based on your own ranking or a site Like FantasyPros. The analyzer sums each side and compares, factoring in the number of players.

Does this work for all fantasy formats?+

Yes. It is format-agnostic — you supply the values, so it works for 9-cat, points leagues, or keeper formats.

Should I trust the winner label?+

It is a starting point, not gospel. Consider roster needs, schedule, and injuries before accepting any trade.
