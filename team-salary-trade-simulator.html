For developers: extending the rule engine

Trades are validated by a registry of pure rule functions in lib/trade-rules.ts. Each rule receives a context with both teams, outgoing/incoming players, salaries, apron status, cash, and picks, then returns a pass / fail / warning / info check with a human-readable reason.

import { registerRule } from "@/lib/trade-rules";

registerRule({
  id: "my-league-rule",
  title: "My house rule",
  team: "both",
  evaluate: (ctx) => ({
    id: "my-league-rule",
    title: "My house rule",
    team: "both",
    status: ctx.afterA > 200 ? "fail" : "pass",
    message: "Team A must stay under $200M",
  }),
});

Call evaluateTrade(teams, proposal, league) to run all registered rules. Built-in rules cover roster size, salary matching, cash limits, no-trade clauses, apron hard caps, and draft-pick inventory. Reset with resetRules().

Frequently asked questions

How does salary matching work?+

Teams below the first apron can take back 200% + $250K of outgoing salary under $7.5M, outgoing + $7.5M between $7.5M and $29M, or 125% + $250K above $29M. Apron teams are limited to roughly 100% matching.

What are the first and second aprons?+

Thresholds above the tax line that trigger roster restrictions. For 2026–27 the first apron is $209.015M and the second apron is $221.686M. Second-apron teams face the harshest limits, including no cash trades and no salary aggregation.

Can I add my own rules?+

Yes. The rule engine is a registry of pure functions. Developers can call registerRule() with a custom evaluate function — see the developer note at the bottom of the tool.

Is the data real?+

The player salaries are real 2026–27 cap figures sourced from HoopsHype and pulled on 19 Nov 2026; league thresholds are the official NBA 2026–27 numbers. Only the top 10–12 contracts per team are listed, so a team's editable roster total runs a little below its official cap hit — both figures are shown. Every value can be edited.

Does this cover every CBA rule?+

No. It models the major headline rules. Real trades also involve BYC, trade exceptions, poison-pill contracts, the Stepien rule and more. Always consult official sources.
