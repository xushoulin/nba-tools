Frequently asked questions

What is Pythagorean expectation?+

A formula by Bill James that estimates a team's expected winning percentage from points scored and points allowed. For basketball the exponent is typically around 13.9.

Why does it matter?+

A team's point differential is often a better predictor of future performance than its actual record, which can be skewed by close games.

Can I model a full season?+

Yes. Enter full-season totals for a complete projection, or partial-season totals to see the current pace.
