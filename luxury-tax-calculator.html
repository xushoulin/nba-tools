Frequently asked questions

How is NBA luxury tax calculated?+

Luxury tax uses progressive brackets. For each $5 million a team is over the tax line, the per-dollar rate increases — starting at $1.50 per dollar and climbing to $3.75 or more for large overages. Repeat taxpayers pay a $0.50 surcharge per bracket.

What is the first and second apron?+

The aprons are thresholds above the tax line that trigger roster-building restrictions. The first apron limits certain transactions; the second apron imposes much harsher limits including frozen draft picks.

Who receives luxury tax money?+

Tax revenue is distributed among teams that stay below the tax line, and a portion goes to the league.
